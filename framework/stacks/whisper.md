Open-weights speech recognition that runs anywhere, including inside a
boundary -- which is the point: voice input arrives from exactly the
engagements (field operations, rural advisory, call centres) whose audio
must often not leave. Multilingual by training, with the standing caveat
that per-language word error rates vary widely and must be measured on the
client's own callers before any number is quoted.
