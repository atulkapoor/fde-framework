1 · A paragraph becomes typed facts (plus the questions worth asking next)

$ fde start acme --statement "Extract fields from scanned supplier invoices; data
  cannot leave; 200,000 documents, 8,000 verified; a person is waiting."
Here is what I took from that:
  - What arrives, and in what form: scanned documents
  - Can client data leave their environment: cannot leave
  - How many items in total: 200,000
  - How many are verified or labelled: 8,000
  - Is a person waiting for the result: yes
worth asking next (fde ask acme --role <who>):
  - Who may use this -- one operating team, distinct roles, or anyone internal?  [admin/sponsor]
  - What availability does this need -- always on, business hours, best effort?   [sponsor/admin]

2 · The build refuses until the engagement is real

$ fde build acme --out project
refused: gates unsatisfied
  [hard] data_access -- credentials have not been shown to work against real data.
      -> Get a connection that returns real rows, then record it:
         `fde data-access acme --note "what returned rows"`. Promised access is not access.
Every gate names its remedy and its clearing command. Waivers ship in RISKS.md, with reasons.

3 · Seat economics, before your seed round subsidizes the discovery

$ fde cost --price-per-seat 25 --workflows-per-day 8
unit economics at $25.00/seat, 8 workflows/day, 5 step(s):
  $0.0225/workflow -> $3.96/seat-month in model spend
  margin: $21.04/seat
  lever: route the measurable share to rules first (cascade at 50%%)  -> margin $23.02
  lever: cache the shared prompt prefix (70%% at the cached rate)     -> margin $23.53
  lever: bound the loop at 3 steps                                   -> margin $22.62

4 · A coding agent finishes the build — against an exam it cannot edit

$ fde implement project --holdout acme/artifacts/holdout.jsonl
round 1: red (6 file(s) changed)
round 2: green
  golden         33 cases  100.0%
  edge_case       8 cases  100.0%
  adversarial     3 cases  100.0%
holdout: green (cases the implementer never saw)
The evals, boundary and decision documents are hashed; an agent that edits them is caught and reverted.

How it fits together

What a Forward Deployed Engineer gets

🔍 Discovery that compounds

Prose, PDFs, sample pairs, role-scoped interviews and hardware scans feed one profile. Provenance decides conflicts; disagreement between people is a finding, not an error.

🚧 Seven gates before building

Verified data access can never be waived. Every waiver ships in the delivered RISKS.md with its reason and your name.

🧾 Decisions with receipts

Simplest applicable approach per component, cited evidence, named rejected alternatives. Deterministic to the fingerprint.

📦 A real project out

Pipeline with approval gates and critics, three-layer evals, runbooks, SLOs, compliance docs. On-prem, hybrid, customer-VPC, air-gapped.

🎛️ Multi-modal by design

Photos AND documents AND telemetry — or voice, or video — fan one perception path per modality, each decided by its own rules.

🤖 LLMs propose, never decide

Reading briefs, mining vocabulary, implementing against the fenced harness, judging output after human calibration. The decision path stays deterministic.

Who it is for

Forward Deployed Engineers, AI consultants and solutions engineers delivering LLM systems into enterprise environments — especially where data residency, air-gapped deployment, unit economics and evaluation ownership decide whether the engagement survives contact with production.
