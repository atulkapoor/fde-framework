Each incoming customer complaint needs a decision: refund, escalate, or
reply with an explanation. The decision must reach three external systems:
the CRM, billing, and the email platform. About 5,000 past complaints carry
a labelled final decision. Runs in the customer VPC; data may leave to the
company's own cloud. Complaints arrive all day; the queue is worked in
batches, nobody is waiting on a screen.
